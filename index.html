    HVAC units

    Duty / standby pair serving Cold Rooms A & B · automatic failover

    Control strategy

    • Controlled value is the warmest cold room probe (worst-case protection).
    • Above setpoint the duty unit ramps load up; below setpoint it ramps down. Standby shares load in normal operation.
    • If the controlled value leaves the deadband, both units go to 100%.
    • Loss of comms or a trip on the duty unit starts the standby unit automatically, raises a critical alarm and notifies the on-call chain.
    • Duty rotation weekly to equalise runtime hours. Monthly failover test logged in Maintenance.
